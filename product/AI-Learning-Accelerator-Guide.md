## Module 1: The 3 Rules of Smart AI Learning

1. **Always give context** – Tell the AI who you are, what level, and the goal.  
2. **Ask for structure** – Request outlines, summaries, quizzes, analogies.  
3. **Verify + apply** – Never copy-paste blindly. Test the knowledge yourself.

## Module 2: Core Prompts (50+ Ready-to-Use)

### Study & Understanding
- “Explain [topic] to me like I’m 12, then like I’m a university student. Use analogies from everyday life.”
- “Create a one-page summary of [chapter/book] with key concepts, definitions, and 5 practice questions.”
- “Turn this text into flashcards (Question | Answer format): [paste text]”
- “What are the 3 most common mistakes people make when learning [topic]? How do I avoid them?”

### Memorization & Retention
- “Generate a spaced-repetition schedule for these 20 concepts over 30 days.”
- “Create a mnemonic story that links these ideas: [list]”
- “Quiz me on [topic]. Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer before continuing.”

### Writing & Assignments
- “Help me outline an essay on [topic]. Include thesis, 3 main points with evidence, and conclusion.”
- “Improve this paragraph for clarity and academic tone: [paste]”
- “Generate 5 different thesis statements for [prompt] ranked by strength.”

### Productivity & Planning
- “Build a realistic weekly study plan for someone with [hours] available who needs to master [subjects].”
- “Break this big project into daily 45-minute tasks with deadlines.”

### Content Creation (School projects, presentations, social)
- “Create a 7-slide presentation outline on [topic] with speaker notes.”
- “Write a short script for a 60-second educational video explaining [concept].”
